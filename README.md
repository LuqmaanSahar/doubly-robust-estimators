# D300_Coursework
Coursework for the D300 module in Mphil Economics and Data Science


I aim to investigate how Doubly Robust learners perform as overlap and treatment prevalence vary. I am basing my analysis on: Yang, Chengxin, Laine E. Thomas, and Fan Li. "Demystify Doubly-Robust Estimation: The Role of Overlap." arXiv preprint arXiv:2602.01648 (2026). 

Who have already explored the role of overlap, but I am slightly extending the analysis to take a deeper look at how their results may vary as treatment intesity also varies. For instance, when a treatment is very rare (e.g. only 5% of the population) vs treatment very common (95%). This is a useful addition because the authors originally only explore two cases (10% and 40% prevalence).
